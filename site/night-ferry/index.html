Design decisions

Put transfer time before the timetable

The most important number is the gap between boats. Keep the walk time beside it and say plainly when the later option is too tight.

Keep the two options easy to compare

Departure, connection, and arrival share the same positions in both states. The mobile layout places the choice above the transfer result and full timetable.

Prototype checks Both departure states, the transfer calculation, keyboard and touch selection, local saving after reload, and reduced motion.
